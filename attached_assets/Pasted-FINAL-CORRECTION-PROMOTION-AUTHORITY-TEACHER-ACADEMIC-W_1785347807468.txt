==================================================
PART 1: FINAL PROMOTION WORKFLOW
==================================================

Promotion must follow this exact workflow:

1. Assigned teachers enter academic marks for their assigned classes and subjects.
2. Teachers submit completed academic results.
3. Academic Administrator reviews the submitted results.
4. Proprietor gives final approval to the academic results.
5. The system calculates promotion recommendations using only approved final results.
6. Academic Administrator prepares the promotion decision.
7. Proprietor approves or rejects the promotion decision.
8. The system changes the learner’s class only after Proprietor approval.

Available decisions:

- PROMOTE
- REPEAT
- FORCE_PROMOTE
- GRADUATE
- DEFER

The system recommendation must consider the school’s configured policy, including where applicable:

- approved annual average
- number of subjects passed
- number of subjects failed
- compulsory subjects passed or failed
- attendance percentage
- completion of all required results
- final result approval status

Do not calculate promotion from:

- draft results
- unsubmitted results
- unreviewed results
- rejected results
- incomplete academic records
- results without Proprietor approval

==================================================
PART 2: FORCE PROMOTION
==================================================

Force promotion must follow these rules:

1. Force promotion applies to one individual learner at a time.
2. It must not be included in automatic batch promotion.
3. A written reason is compulsory.
4. The original system recommendation must remain visible.
5. Display:
   - approved marks
   - annual average
   - subjects passed
   - subjects failed
   - compulsory-subject outcome
   - attendance percentage
6. Academic Administrator recommends FORCE_PROMOTE.
7. Proprietor approves or rejects the request.
8. The learner’s class must not change before Proprietor approval.
9. Record permanently:
   - learner
   - academic year
   - current class
   - proposed next class
   - original system recommendation
   - force-promotion reason
   - requesting Academic Administrator
   - approving Proprietor
   - request date
   - approval or rejection date
   - application date
10. Create a permanent audit entry.
11. Do not permit force promotion without a written reason.
12. Do not allow batch force promotion.
13. Do not allow the Academic Administrator to approve their own recommendation.

==================================================
PART 3: NORMAL BATCH PROMOTION
==================================================

1. Support batch promotion for learners who satisfy the configured policy.
2. Exclude learners with incomplete, rejected, draft, or unapproved results.
3. Exclude FORCE_PROMOTE cases from normal batches.
4. Academic Administrator prepares the batch.
5. Proprietor approves and applies the batch.
6. Preserve historical:
   - classes
   - academic years
   - attendance
   - marks
   - reports
   - approvals
   - promotion decisions
7. Do not overwrite previous academic-year records.
8. Learners in the final class must be marked COMPLETED or GRADUATED.
9. Repeating learners remain in their current class for the new academic year.
10. Deferred learners remain unchanged until a later approved decision.

PART 4: FINAL PROMOTION ROLE MATRIX
==================================================

ACADEMIC_ADMIN

- review approved academic records
- prepare promotion recommendations
- recommend PROMOTE, REPEAT, FORCE_PROMOTE, GRADUATE, or DEFER
- select the proposed next class
- provide the compulsory written reason for FORCE_PROMOTE
- prepare normal promotion batches
- cannot give final approval
- cannot apply a promotion before Proprietor approval
- cannot alter original teacher marks

PROPRIETOR

- review the Academic Administrator’s recommendations
- approve or reject individual promotion decisions
- approve individual FORCE_PROMOTE requests
- give final approval to normal promotion batches
- apply approved promotions
- cannot create or alter original teacher marks
- cannot approve a promotion that has not been prepared by the Academic Administrator

HEADTEACHER

- view approved academic records
- view system promotion recommendations
- monitor promotion progress
- view approved and rejected promotion decisions
- cannot prepare promotion recommendations
- cannot recommend FORCE_PROMOTE
- cannot approve or reject promotion decisions
- cannot apply promotion batches

SCHOOL_ADMIN

- view promotion progress and reports only
- cannot prepare or recommend promotion decisions
- cannot force-promote
- cannot approve, reject, or apply promotions

SUPER_ADMIN

- configure promotion policy
- view and audit all promotion activities
- exceptionally reopen a completed promotion decision with a compulsory reason
- cannot perform routine promotion recommendations
- cannot approve or apply ordinary promotion decisions

TEACHER

- view academic records and promotion recommendations for officially assigned classes and subjects
- cannot recommend, approve, reject, force-promote, or apply promotion decisions

PART 6: OFFICIAL CLASS TEACHER
==================================================

An official Class Teacher may:

- create and publish general homework for the officially assigned class
- create and record general classwork
- create and record general class exercises
- create general class activities
- view learners in the assigned class
- record learner submission status
- record submitted-late status
- mark general academic work they created
- record scores and maximum scores
- record teacher remarks
- record submission and marking dates
- monitor completed, unmarked, and outstanding work for the assigned class

The Class Teacher must not create subject-specific work for a subject they are not assigned to teach.

When the Class Teacher is also officially assigned to teach a subject, they may create and mark work for that subject.

==================================================
PART 7: ASSIGNED SUBJECT TEACHER
==================================================

An assigned Subject Teacher may:

- create and publish homework for the assigned subject and class
- create and record classwork for the assigned subject
- create and record class exercises for the assigned subject
- record lessons or academic work completed for that subject where supported
- record learner submission status
- record submitted-late status
- mark homework, classwork, and exercises for that subject
- record scores and maximum scores
- record teacher remarks
- record submission and marking dates
- view performance for assigned learners and subjects

A Subject Teacher must record only work belonging to their officially assigned subject and class.

A Subject Teacher must not:

- create work for an unassigned subject
- create work for an unassigned class
- mark another teacher’s subject work
- alter another teacher’s scores or remarks

PART 8: HEADTEACHER ACADEMIC WORK
==================================================

A Headteacher may be an active teacher.

The system must allow a Headteacher to be officially assigned:

- one class as the official Class Teacher
- all subjects in that class, or
- selected subjects, such as three or four subjects

HEADTEACHER AS CLASS TEACHER

When officially assigned as the Class Teacher, the Headteacher may:

- create and publish general homework for the assigned class
- create and record general classwork
- create and record general class exercises
- create general class activities
- record learner submission status
- mark general work they created
- record scores, maximum scores, remarks, submission dates, and marking dates
- monitor every learner in the assigned class

HEADTEACHER AS SUBJECT TEACHER

For every subject officially assigned to the Headteacher, they may:

- create and publish subject homework
- create and record subject classwork
- create and record subject class exercises
- mark learner work for that subject
- record scores and teacher remarks
- update submission and marking records
- view subject performance for assigned learners

HEADTEACHER ASSIGNMENT RULES

1. Being a Headteacher alone must not grant teaching access to every class and subject.
2. Teaching permissions must come from official class and subject assignments.
3. When assigned all subjects in one class, the Headteacher may teach, create work, and mark all those subjects.
4. When assigned only selected subjects, the Headteacher must be restricted to those subjects.
5. The Headteacher must not create work for an unrelated class.
6. The Headteacher must not mark another teacher’s subject work.

HEADTEACHER SCHOOL-WIDE MANAGEMENT FUNCTIONS

In addition to their own teaching assignments, the Headteacher may:

- monitor homework, classwork, and exercises throughout the school
- review completion and marking reports
- identify unmarked or overdue academic work
- monitor teacher publishing and marking performance
- return inappropriate work for correction
- request corrections through the authorised workflow

School-wide monitoring must not grant permission to alter another teacher’s marks or remarks.

==================================================
PART 9: ACADEMIC ADMINISTRATOR
==================================================

The Academic Administrator may:

- monitor homework, classwork, exercises, and general class activities
- review teacher publishing and marking activity
- return inappropriate academic work for correction
- request a correction to a score or remark
- monitor missing submissions
- monitor unmarked work
- view school-wide academic-work reports

The Academic Administrator may create and mark academic work only when officially assigned as the relevant Class Teacher or Subject Teacher.

The Academic Administrator’s management role alone must not grant teaching permission.

PART 11: LEARNER SUBMISSION AND MARKING
==================================================

Learners do not have AcademiaOS login accounts.

Teachers must record submission and marking for every learner.

Support these learner-level statuses:

- NOT_SUBMITTED
- SUBMITTED
- SUBMITTED_LATE
- EXCUSED
- MARKED

For every learner record store:

- learner ID
- academic work ID
- class ID
- subject ID where applicable
- teacher ID
- submission status
- submission date
- late flag
- score received
- maximum score
- teacher remark
- marked-by teacher ID
- marked date
- correction reason where applicable
- attachment or evidence reference where supported
- created and updated timestamps

VALIDATION

1. Score must not exceed the maximum score.
2. Score and maximum score must not be negative.
3. MARKED status requires a valid score and maximum score.
4. A marked record must identify the marking teacher and date.
5. An EXCUSED learner may have no score.
6. Corrections after marking require a written reason.
7. Preserve the original score and remark in the audit log.
8. An unassigned user must not mark or change the record.
