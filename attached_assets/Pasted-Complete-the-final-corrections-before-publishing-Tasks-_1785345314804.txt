1. SUPER ADMIN RESTRICTIONS

Super Admin must not perform routine academic work.

For homework:
- Super Admin may view, audit, unpublish inappropriate content and perform exceptional moderation.
- Super Admin must not routinely create, edit or publish homework.
- Remove any general assignment bypass that allows Super Admin to publish homework.
- Exceptional moderation must not be treated as teacher authorship.

For promotion:
- Super Admin may configure promotion policy, view all recommendations, audit decisions and exceptionally reopen a completed promotion batch.
- Super Admin must not record routine Promote, Repeat, Force Promote, Graduate or Defer decisions.
- Super Admin must not approve or apply an ordinary promotion batch.
- Headteacher or Academic Administrator records the recommendation decision.
- Proprietor gives final approval and applies the batch.

2. CURRICULUM TOPIC PERMISSIONS

Only a teacher officially assigned to the exact class and subject may create curriculum topics.

- HEADTEACHER and ACADEMIC_ADMIN may create a topic only when they also have the relevant teacher assignment.
- Otherwise, they may review, monitor, return or remove topics but cannot create them.
- SCHOOL_ADMIN and PROPRIETOR remain view-only.
- Enforce this in server actions and direct requests.

3. TEACHER PROMOTION VISIBILITY

Teachers must be able to view promotion recommendations only for classes where they are:
- the official Class Teacher, or
- otherwise explicitly authorised for that class.

Teachers cannot:
- record the final promotion decision,
- force-promote,
- approve,
- apply a promotion batch.

Update the promotion role matrix accordingly.
