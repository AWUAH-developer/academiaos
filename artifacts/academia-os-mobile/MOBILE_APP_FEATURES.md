# AcademiaOS Mobile 1.0 Feature Map

## Parents and guardians
- Linked children only
- Attendance history
- Fee balances and charges
- Payment receipts
- Approved results
- Published terminal reports
- Announcements and alerts

## Teachers and authorised staff
- Assigned or school-authorised learners
- Learner profile photos
- Attendance history
- Mobile attendance entry when permission allows
- Approved academic information
- Device and password management

## Proprietors and administrators
- School-wide authorised learner visibility
- Approved-result and report visibility
- Fee and attendance summaries according to role
- Notifications and announcements

## Web relationship
The existing Next.js Replit application remains the complete administration portal. The Expo web target is a lightweight family and staff companion, not a replacement for complex school setup, payroll, timetable configuration, user administration, or bulk academic processing.
